Problem Formulation & Real-World Mess

Speaker 2: Data & Baseline Engineer (Duration: 1:30 min)
  • Benchmark Dataset: 118 real pavement and concrete images (CrackForest CFD) with pixel-level ground truth masks.
  • Severe Real-World Imaging Mess:

    1. Low Contrast

    Dark aggregate stones overlap in intensity with thin hairline crack grooves.

    2. Uneven Illumination

    Sunlight gradients and bridge shadows defeat single global thresholds.

    3. Pores & Texture

    Granular concrete roughness creates dense false-positive speckle.

  • Primary Metric Justification: IoU (Jaccard Index) is mandatory because cracks occupy <2% of surface area. Pixel accuracy would be deceptively >98% on an all-background trivial mask.

Systematic Parameter Tuning & 5 Ablation Studies

Speaker 4 & 5: Restoration & Segmentation Engineers (Duration: 1:30 min)
  • 23 Experimental Runs Logged: Parameterized OFAT sweeps (smoothing kernels, cutoff D0, SE geometry).
  • Key Scientific Findings from Ablation Studies:
    • Ablation 2 (Frequency vs Spatial Blur): Butterworth low-pass gained +6.53% IoU over spatial Gaussian blur by preserving directional crack edges.
    • Ablation 3 (Noise Restoration): Median filter achieved 29.83 dB PSNR vs 25.46 dB for arithmetic mean under 3% impulse noise (+4.37 dB gain).
    • Ablation 5 (Morphological Refinement): Closing + Small Object removal more than doubled the IoU (0.1537 → 0.3191) by pruning false-positive pore noise.

    Pipeline C (Final)

    IoU: 0.2448 (>5× baseline)
    Area Error: 1.86% (18× reduction)
    Time: 71.3 ms

  • Failure Analysis on Worst 20 Cases:
    • Sub-pixel Hairlines (<0.15% area): Too thin; smoothed by low-pass filter. Fix: Multi-scale Hessian ridge detection.
    • Deep Structural Shadows: Dynamic range collapses. Fix: Homomorphic frequency filtering.
    • Honeycomb Pores: Spherical voids touching cracks. Fix: Shape eccentricity filtering.
